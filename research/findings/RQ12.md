# RQ12: Cost: GPU seconds per audio minute, and what hardware a bank needs

**Answer in one sentence:** speech recognition is cheap (0.8-2.5 GPU-s per audio minute, about 1-4% of real time on
one RTX 4070), and the LLM layer is the cost driver. The recommended setup (large-v3 MIT + Qwen2.5-7B `facts`) runs at
roughly 3-4 GPU-s per audio minute, so one 12 GB GPU covers about 2,000 five-minute calls a day. Caveat: the 7B facts
part is an estimate, because its throughput was never timed alone. (confidence: high for ASR, medium for the LLM
stages, low for the sizing extrapolation)

## Setup
- One RTX 4070 12 GB, 16 cores, driver 535; faster-whisper / CTranslate2 for ASR; vLLM 0.10.1.1 for the LLM;
  diarization sherpa-onnx on CPU. Sources: research/journal.md timings, runs/asr_eval/*/summary.md (RTF), the cost
  columns of runs/experiments/*/summary.md, docs/OPERATING_CONCEPT.md §8.
- Case audio: 60 calls = 4 h 40 min, average **4.7 min per call**. "Per audio minute" figures for LLM stages are
  derived here as (s per call) / 4.7. These derived numbers are marked "derived".
- Throughput (calls per wall-clock time, stage alone on the GPU) and latency (seconds one call waits under parallel
  load) are different quantities. The experiment summaries report latency. Sizing uses throughput.

## Sizing (from OPERATING_CONCEPT §8, which uses the numbers above)
- Recommended pipeline per average call: large-v3 ~11 s + facts 7B ~3-5 s (estimate) of GPU time; diarization on CPU.
- 2,000 calls/day (~9,400 call minutes): ~9 GPU-hours/day on one RTX 4070-class GPU. One mid-range GPU suffices, and
  a second gives redundancy.
- ASR alone: 10,000 audio minutes/day need ~2.5 (turbo) to ~7 (large-v3) GPU-hours.
- 14B LLM: needs the GPU alone on 12 GB (run ASR first, then the LLM). It costs several times the 7B per call
  (journal 03:10: "3x cost") for no detectable gain (RQ6). A 24-48 GB card (L4 / L40S) runs ASR + a 14-30B model side by side.
- One 12 GB card holds vLLM (~66% of memory) plus one Whisper turbo, but **not** vLLM plus large-v3 fp16 (OOM at
  bring-up). The recommended setup therefore runs staged (scripts/run_staged.sh: ASR, then LLM).

## Recommendation
Pitch the cost as "transcription costs ~1-4% of real time on one gaming-class GPU; the language model is the cost
driver, and the fact-question design keeps it small (single-token answers, prefix caching)". Before Sunday, time 7B
`facts` alone on 48 calls (one run, ~minutes) to replace the estimate.

## Strongest counter-argument
The facts-on-7B cost is an estimate, and the latency figures from the summaries (13 s per call) are 3-4x higher than
the throughput estimate. If the real throughput sits near the latency figure, the daily budget roughly doubles (still
one GPU). Not measured: the cascade (A2: cheap checks first, LLM only on suspicious calls), batching effects at bank
scale, CPU cost of diarization at 2,000 calls/day (~10 CPU-hours at 18 s per call, parallel over cores), and real
call lengths.

## What would change our mind
A clean timing of facts-7B above ~15 s per call. Or production audio far longer than the case average of 4.7 min.
